How Protomates Works

ENGINEERING
PROCESS

01

Requirement Scoping & Architecture

We analyze your project goals, academic guidelines (IEEE/University), or product specifications. We define the optimal hardware platform (ESP32, Arduino, ARM), sensor selections, and budget BOM.

Phase 1
02

Circuit Schematic & PCB Layout

Our EDA engineers build electrical schematics, component footprints, routing topologies, and multi-layer PCB design templates with verified Gerber fabrication files.

Phase 2
03

Firmware & AI Algorithm Integration

We write clean, modular embedded C++/Python code, configure RTOS tasks, integrate sensor drivers, and train or deploy edge AI/ML models on target hardware.

Phase 3
04

Hardware Prototyping & Bench Testing

Physical prototype assembly, board soldering, power plane verification, signal debugging, and end-to-end performance stress testing to guarantee rock-solid reliability.

Phase 4
05

Delivery, Reports & Viva Guidance

You receive the fully working prototype, source code repository, documentation reports, PPT presentations, and comprehensive 1-on-1 viva defense coaching.
